## 🌌 About Zenrick

**Zenrick** is a private technology ecosystem dedicated to pushing the boundaries of autonomous systems, scalable infrastructure, and intelligent automation. We build the foundational engines that power next-generation B2B platforms, AI agents, and high-performance microservices.

Our philosophy is rooted in **sovereign infrastructure** and **event-driven architecture**, orchestrating everything from large language models (LLMs) to complex multi-tenant workflows across self-managed bare-metal and VPS clusters.

---

## 🏗️ Core Architecture & Platforms

At the heart of the Zenrick ecosystem are highly specialized engines designed for scale, resilience, and deep AI integration:

### 🧠 The AI Core (Nexus & Kyron)
Our proprietary Agentic AI orchestrator framework.
* **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** Universal standard for context injection and tool execution.
* **Vector Intelligence:** High-performance semantic search and RAG pipelines powered by `pgvector`.
* **Dynamic Schedulers & Workers:** Distributed task orchestration via Redis and Node/Python execution engines.

### 🛡️ Zenrick Identity & API Gateway
The unified entry point and security layer for all ecosystem products.
* **Self-Hosted Identity:** Robust multi-tenant authentication utilizing Supabase GoTrue.
* **Edge Routing:** Traffic orchestration and reverse proxying powered by Traefik and Nginx.
* **Data Sovereignty:** Dedicated, isolated PostgreSQL instances managed through PgBouncer.

### 🧪 Quality & DevOps Orchestration
A culture of continuous reliability and zero-downtime deployments.
* **Automated CI/CD:** Seamless deployment pipelines leveraging Dokploy across distributed clusters.
* **Data-Driven QA:** Integrated testing analytics and reporting dashboards via Allure.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

We build with technologies that guarantee control, performance, and scalability.

- **Infrastructure:** `Docker` `Dokploy` `Traefik` `MinIO` `Cloudflare`
- **Data & AI:** `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `Redis` `TensorFlow` `Ollama` 
- **Backend Core:** `Node.js` `Python` `Supabase Stack` `n8n`
- **Frontend & Interfaces:** `React` `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS`
